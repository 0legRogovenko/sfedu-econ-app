Review combines rendered PDF tables, their printed group columns and time
rows, and exact date/week annotations. The reviewed result contains 39 groups
and 1368 lesson records across both modules. Previous source snapshots remain
available for historical comparisons.

Important source details preserved:

- The same table can give different module end dates for different groups.
- Repeated column headers do not end a continuing teaching day.
- Year-three Monday language teaching has three separate time blocks.
- Saturday MUAM lectures and seminars remain separate lessons.
- Master lessons retain full teacher lists, sparse dates, start/end dates,
  and upper/lower week annotations. The printed `except 2 December` is an
  explicit list of permitted Wednesdays.
- Additional 20:55–21:40 master teaching is stored as a separate eighth slot;
  its exact times are preserved without colliding with the preceding slot.
- The 26–31 October master exception is bounded to that week and supersedes
  that programme's first-module weekly timetable for those dates.
- Group 4.7 remains present; addresses are separated from teacher names.

The PDFs include curriculum statements about examinations, but no dated exam
timetable for these groups. No exam dates have been invented. Unparsed source
fragments remain in the administration queue, including bare VPK placeholders
and curriculum continuation fragments; reviewed additions are traceable to
their source page in `corrections.json`.
